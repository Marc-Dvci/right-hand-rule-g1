RIGHT HAND RULE makes a blindfolded, free-standing Unitree G1 navigate by touch. Joint state and pelvis IMU are the complete input set. Navigation is scripted; walking/balance use attributed pretrained NVIDIA GR00T networks.

The frozen primary evaluation recorded **94/100 exits**, zero falls and zero checker findings at nominal timing, over 14 distinct maze topologies with randomized dynamics/sensors. Neutral, touch-off and delayed-touch controls each exited 0/10; the complete controller exited 9/10 on those matched seeds. The exact ARM64 image passed **three standard and three task runs**, with every native task exit independently scored from the kit trace. [Native workflow and raw artifacts](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/right-hand-rule-g1/actions/runs/42656538931).

The contest files are `RightHandRule.zip` and `result.mp4`. The larger `submission.tar`, `maze-trajectories.zip` and `native-arm64-evidence.zip` are separate downloads. `SHA256SUMS`, `artifacts.json` and `package-check.json` identify and verify the bytes. The ZIP contains the full source, pinned dependencies, runner reference, all final episode reports, replay data, independent model parity fixtures, audits and hardware notes. Run `./run.sh` on Linux/Python 3.12 after installing requirements.
